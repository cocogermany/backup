How the Referral Program Works

Earn rewards in three simple steps with complete privacy.

1

Invite Friends

Copy your unique link and share it with friends preparing for Goethe-Zertifikat or telc exams (A1–B2).

2

They Join

When your friend creates an account, they are permanently attributed to your profile with zero manual setup.

3

Earn 10% in Coins

Whenever your referred learner purchases an eligible plan, 10% commission is credited to your coin balance automatically.

Privacy Notice: We never display names, email addresses, or personal learning data of your referred friends. Only aggregated counts and transaction coin amounts are shown.
